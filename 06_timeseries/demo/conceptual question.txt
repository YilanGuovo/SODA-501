A random train/test split may cause time leakage that the model may use information from the future during training. Because what it should predict is used for training, the model performance may look better than it really is. in real life, we only use past data to predict the future. Train on past and test on future is the default because it corresponds to the real situations that we use past data to predict the future. As for tolling-origin (backtesting), Instead of testing only once, it moves the time window forward and test many times. This helps us check whether the model is stable across different time periods, not just good at one point in time.
